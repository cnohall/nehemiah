# Impeccable Design Context — Nehemiah: The Wall

## Design Context

### Users
Cooperative players (2–4), likely friends or family playing together on PC. Sessions are focused and purposeful — they came to play a game with meaning. Many players may share the creator's faith background (Jehovah's Witnesses). Context: seated at home, group energy, willing to engage with historical and spiritual depth. Not casual clickers — they want to feel the weight of what they're doing.

### Brand Personality
**Sacred · Urgent · Communal**

- **Sacred**: The work has divine purpose. Every wall section matters. The interface should never feel flippant.
- **Urgent**: 52 days. Enemies are coming. UI must communicate stakes without anxiety-inducing clutter.
- **Communal**: Built together. UI should feel warm and human, not sterile or corporate.

Emotional goal: Players should feel like they are part of something important — not just playing a game, but participating in a story that matters.

### Visual Reference
Octopath Traveler / Triangle Strategy — HD-2D aesthetic:
- Warm, painterly lighting with real depth
- Cinematic presentation with historical grounding
- UI that feels handcrafted, not templated
- Menus that feel like illuminated manuscripts or carved stone, not software dashboards

**Anti-references**: Dark gothic dungeon crawlers, neon cyber aesthetics, generic fantasy RPG chrome, anything that feels gratuitous or combat-glorifying.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Theme**: Warm light — Jerusalem at sunrise. Open sky, warm stone, morning amber.

**Palette (OKLCH)**:
- Background: `oklch(96% 0.01 80)` — warm cream
- Surface / parchment: `oklch(92% 0.018 75)` — aged parchment
- Text primary: `oklch(22% 0.02 70)` — deep warm charcoal
- Text secondary: `oklch(45% 0.03 65)` — warm mid-brown
- Accent / amber: `oklch(58% 0.13 65)` — terracotta/burnt amber
- Accent warm: `oklch(68% 0.11 82)` — warm gold
- Accent olive: `oklch(52% 0.09 128)` — muted olive

Tint all neutrals toward hue ~70–80 (amber). Even `oklch(96% 0.008 78)` reads warmer than pure gray.

**Typography**:
- Display / headings: **Cinzel** (Google Fonts) — lapidary Roman inscriptions, sacred and timeless. Not on reflex reject list. Perfect fit: stone-carved, authoritative, ancient without being illegible.
- Body / UI labels: **Spectral** (Google Fonts) — refined serif, slightly editorial, excellent at small sizes.
- Avoid: anything on the reflex_fonts_to_reject list, monospace as a "historical" shorthand, heavy gothic blackletter.

**Texture & motif**: Stone, parchment, worn edges. Subtle grain textures on surfaces where appropriate. Dividers can use aged-stone horizontal rules. No glassmorphism, no drop shadows on everything.

### Design Principles

1. **Weight before decoration** — Hierarchy comes from scale and spacing, not ornament. A wall section label should feel important because of its position and weight, not because it has a glowing border.

2. **Dignity over polish** — This is not a AAA game trying to look expensive. It should look intentional. Handcrafted over slick. Think illuminated manuscript, not tech startup.

3. **HUD serves the game, not itself** — In-game UI should be minimal enough to disappear when you're not looking for it. Health, materials, day — present but never loud.

4. **Warmth is structural** — The warm palette isn't decoration. It's the emotional foundation. Every surface, every shadow, every divider should feel like it's made of warm stone or aged parchment.

5. **Scripture is flavor, not feature** — NWT quotes are ambient. They reinforce tone. They never interrupt or demand attention.
