Rebuild the wall of Jerusalem with your friends before the enemy breaks through.

It is 455 BCE. Jerusalem's wall lies in ruins, and Nehemiah has come home to raise it again. You and up to three friends are the crew: carry wood, stone and mortar, work the wall up course by course, and sling stones at the raiders who come to stop you. Builders have their hands full, so someone always has to stand guard, just as in Nehemiah 4:17.

★ CO-OP FOR 2–4
Host a game and share the five-letter room code. Friends join from their own phones. Playing alone? Computer crew members fill the empty places.

★ CARRY, BUILD, DEFEND
Every stretch of wall goes up in three steps: timber, stone, mortar. Deliver the loads, then stand at the wall and work it up. More hands build faster, and a hit stops the work.

★ WAVES AND NIGHTFALL
Enemies come in waves: a bell warns you, a pointer shows where they'll hit. Every day ends when the stars come out, and each section must stand before nightfall on its last day.

★ WATCH POSTS
Raise watch posts behind the wall and keep their slingers stocked with stone. Every stone in a post is a stone not in the wall. Choose well.

★ 52 DAYS, 12 GATES
Work your way around the city gate by gate, from the Sheep Gate to the Miphkad Gate, in the order of Nehemiah chapter 3. Every section brings its own twist: heavy beams carried in pairs, stone salvaged from burned rubble, mortar mixed at the trough, a night watch, messengers trying to lure you away.

★ GROUNDED IN THE ACCOUNT
Short story cards between sections follow the book of Nehemiah, with verses from the World English Bible. Earn up to three marks on each section and replay any stretch from the map.

★ PLAY YOUR WAY
English, Spanish, Portuguese, German and Korean. No ads, no in-app purchases.
